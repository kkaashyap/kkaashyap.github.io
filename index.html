I am a Master's student at Georgia Tech specializing in Machine Learning, advised by Prof. Animesh Garg. I am also fortunate to work with Prof. Shreyas Kousik and Prof. James Hays.

I research generalist embodied agents that can adapt to unstructured environments using large-scale representation learning and multi-modal generative models. I am specifically interested in scaling robot learning to internet-scale data and searching for better spatial priors for robotics.

Previously, I've worked at Databricks and Amazon, and was an undergraduate researcher at the Generative Graphics Group working with Prof. Bo Zhu developing physically grounded generative models to accelerate high dimensional scientific simulation and discovery.
